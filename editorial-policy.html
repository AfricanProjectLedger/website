Editorial principles

APL prioritises accuracy, relevance, independence and clarity. We distinguish reported fact from analysis and avoid presenting speculation as certainty.

Sources and verification

We draw from government releases, company disclosures, financing institutions, procurement notices, regulatory sources and credible reporting. Material claims are cross-checked where practicable.

Corrections

Substantive errors are corrected promptly and transparently. Readers may contact the research desk at research@africanprojectledger.com.

Commercial separation

Memberships, partnerships, recruitment work and sponsored engagements do not determine APL’s independent editorial conclusions. Commercial content will be identified clearly.
