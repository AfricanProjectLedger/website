Dangote advances 700,000 BPD Kenya refinery with $450m-plus engineering mandate

Engineers India has signed a contract worth more than $450 million to act as project-management and EPCM consultant on Dangote’s planned integrated refinery and petrochemical complex in Kenya.

Executive brief

India’s state-owned Engineers India Limited has formally extended its delivery partnership with Dangote Group into East Africa. Under the signed agreement, EIL will provide project-management consultancy and engineering, procurement and construction-management services for a 700,000-barrel-a-day greenfield refinery and petrochemical plant in Kenya.

The mandate follows EIL’s work on Dangote’s 650,000-barrel-a-day Lekki refinery in Nigeria and its expansion. The official disclosure values EIL’s Kenya contract at more than $450 million, providing a material execution signal for a project intended to serve East African demand, reduce petroleum-product import dependence and supply export markets.

Why this is APL-worthy

This is more than a feasibility announcement: a major engineering and project-management contract has been signed for what would be one of Africa’s largest new downstream assets. The scale would materially reshape East African fuel supply, logistics and import flows while generating a long-duration pipeline across engineering, construction, storage, marine infrastructure and supporting utilities.

What to watch

The next bankability tests are the full capital structure, environmental and regulatory approvals, site-enabling works, procurement packages and a confirmed construction schedule. Evidence of financing close and physical mobilisation will determine how quickly the engineering mandate converts into delivery.
