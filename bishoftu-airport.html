APL Executive Brief

Aviation project, multi-sector platform

Ethiopian Airlines officially commenced construction of Bishoftu International Airport on 10 January 2026. The planned four-runway hub is designed for capacity of up to 110 million passengers annually. The reported project cost has risen to approximately US$12.5 billion as scope expanded.

The opportunity extends beyond terminal construction into airfield systems, baggage handling, utilities, roads, hospitality, logistics, cargo, urban development and specialised project leadership. Ethiopian Airlines’ July 2026 recruitment for airport-planning, baggage-handling and engineering expertise is a practical activation signal.

Capital Position

Ethiopian Airlines is expected to fund 30% and seek the balance from international lenders. AfDB previously indicated a planned US$500 million commitment, subject to approval, while acting to mobilise a much larger financing syndication. Financing remains a key proof point and should not be described as fully closed.

Who wins—and where the money is

  • Main works, earthworks and airfield contractors
  • Terminal, baggage, security and airport-systems providers
  • Road, utilities, cargo and logistics developers
  • Design, project management and lenders’ advisers
  • Hospitality, commercial real estate and specialised technical talent

Project Friction Monitor

Current watch: financing closure, main works awards, delivery coordination at exceptional scale, utilities, surface access, resettlement and schedule control.

Next proof point

Confirmed main construction awards and evidence that international financing moves from mobilisation into committed capital.
