Morocco signs €270m AfDB financing for airport expansion

The African Development Bank and Morocco’s airport operator have signed a €270 million financing agreement to expand and modernise Marrakech, Agadir, Tangier and Fez airports.

Executive brief

The agreement finances Morocco’s Airport Infrastructure Expansion and Modernisation Programme, known as PEMIA. It was signed in Casablanca by African Development Bank country manager Achraf Tarsim and Office National des Aéroports chief executive Adel El Fakir, with a guarantee from Tamwilcom.

The programme covers airport infrastructure, air-navigation systems, safety and security equipment, automated baggage handling and digital passenger services. By 2030, planned annual capacity is expected to reach 14 million passengers at Marrakech, 5 million at Agadir, 3.6 million at Tangier and 3 million at Fez.

Why this is APL-worthy

This is a signed, institutionally backed financing package that converts Morocco’s aviation-capacity strategy into funded upgrades across four strategic gateways. It also creates a defined pipeline for civil works, airport systems, security technology, baggage equipment and digital-infrastructure suppliers ahead of the country’s 2030 tourism and major-event ambitions.

What to watch

Key execution signals will be loan effectiveness and disbursement, procurement and contract awards, construction schedules, and delivery against the four airports’ stated capacity targets.
