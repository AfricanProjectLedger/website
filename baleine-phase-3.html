APL Executive Brief

The signal behind the contract

Eni and its partners approved Baleine Phase 3 in May 2026. On 27 July, Saipem announced new Eni awards in Côte d’Ivoire and Italy worth approximately €800 million in total, including the offshore Baleine scope. The Ivorian work covers subsea engineering, procurement, fabrication, transportation and installation. Separate drilling activity adds another delivery layer.

Phase 3 is designed to lift total Baleine production from roughly 60,000 to 150,000 barrels of oil per day and gas from 80 to 200 million cubic feet per day. All produced gas is intended for Côte d’Ivoire’s domestic market.

Capital Position

The final investment decision is approved and major delivery contracts are being placed. Public disclosures do not yet provide one consolidated Phase 3 capital figure; APL therefore treats individual contract values and disclosed work scopes separately rather than presenting an unsupported total.

Who wins—and where the money is

  • Subsea engineering, fabrication and installation providers
  • Drilling and offshore marine contractors
  • FPSO systems, controls and commissioning specialists
  • Local logistics, base services, inspection and HSE providers
  • Project controls, contract administration and technical talent

Project Friction Monitor

Current watch: fast-track execution complexity, offshore supply-chain sequencing, local-content delivery and coordination across multiple packages. No public evidence reviewed by APL indicates that these risks have stopped the programme.

Next proof point

Visible mobilisation of Phase 3 subsea, drilling and FPSO work packages, followed by confirmed execution milestones.
