About African Project Ledger

An independent intelligence platform built to make Africa’s project economy clearer, more visible and more commercially useful.

Our purpose

African Project Ledger tracks where capital is moving across Africa and translates project announcements into decision-useful intelligence.

What we deliver

APL reports on major infrastructure, construction, energy, transport, mining, industrial, digital and real estate developments. Our coverage connects each signal to the wider commercial ecosystem: engineering, procurement, logistics, investment, executive talent, partnerships and market entry.

Who we serve

Our audience includes executives, investors, developers, contractors, consultants, government stakeholders, development institutions, professional services firms and project leaders.

Our position

APL is Africa-focused, commercially aware and independent. We aim to become a trusted reference point for understanding where major projects are heading and what they may activate next.
