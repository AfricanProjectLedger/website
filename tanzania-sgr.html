Executive brief

Financing is the point at which the corridor can begin activating delivery capacity.

The public signal is the movement of capital around the Tabora–Kigoma standard-gauge railway. The commercial test is no longer announcement volume; it is whether financing converts into procurement, mobilisation and visible construction.
