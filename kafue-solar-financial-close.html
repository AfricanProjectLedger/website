Kafue solar reaches $52.1m financial close in Zambia

Globeleq has closed long-term project financing from FMO and British International Investment for a 40 MWac solar plant backed by a 25-year ZESCO power-purchase agreement.

Executive brief

Globeleq announced financial close on the Kafue Solar Project, a 40 MWac (56 MWp) photovoltaic development near Kafue Town in Lusaka Province. FMO and British International Investment have committed US$52.1 million in long-term project finance: US$27.6 million from FMO and US$24.5 million from BII. Standard Bank South Africa is acting as security trustee.

The project comprises two co-located 20 MWac plants and a 3.5-kilometre transmission line to the Kafue Town substation. It was awarded under Zambia’s GET FiT programme and will sell electricity to ZESCO through a 25-year power-purchase agreement. Globeleq will own the project and manage construction, asset management, operations and maintenance.

Why this is APL-worthy

Financial close converts an eight-year development process into a construction-ready renewable-energy asset. Zambia’s Ministry of Energy identifies Kafue as the first 40 MW solar project under GET FiT Zambia to reach this milestone, making it a useful execution test for the wider 120 MW programme.

What to watch

First debt drawdown, contractor mobilisation, delivery of the grid connection, construction progress and ZESCO’s payment performance under the long-term PPA will determine execution quality.
