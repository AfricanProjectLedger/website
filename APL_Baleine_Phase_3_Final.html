APL Executive Brief

The contract signal confirms the next delivery cycle.

Eni and its partners approved Baleine Phase 3 in May 2026. On 27 July, Saipem announced new Eni awards in Côte d’Ivoire and Italy worth approximately €800 million in total, including the offshore Baleine scope. The Ivorian work covers subsea engineering, procurement, fabrication, transportation and installation. Separate drilling activity adds a second delivery layer.
