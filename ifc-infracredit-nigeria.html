IFC commits $50m to expand InfraCredit’s infrastructure-finance capacity

A signed ten-year subordinated debt facility will strengthen InfraCredit’s capital base and its ability to mobilise long-term naira financing for infrastructure projects.

Executive brief

InfraCredit announced the execution of a US$50 million subordinated unsecured debt facility with the International Finance Corporation. The committed facility is structured in two US$25 million tranches and is supported by the International Development Association Private Sector Window Blended Finance Facility and the Concessional Capital Window.

The transaction is designed to expand InfraCredit’s capacity to support infrastructure financing in renewable energy, digital infrastructure, telecommunications, transportation, healthcare, climate-smart agriculture and other productive sectors. InfraCredit says it has already facilitated more than NGN600 billion in long-term local-currency financing across 28 infrastructure projects.

Why this is APL-worthy

The facility adds loss-absorbing capital to a specialist guarantee institution that connects infrastructure issuers with Nigeria’s domestic pension and institutional capital. Its impact can therefore extend beyond the US$50 million commitment through additional guaranteed local-currency debt.

What to watch

Disbursement of the two tranches, new guarantee mandates, named project financings and the volume of domestic capital mobilised will show how the facility converts into infrastructure delivery.
