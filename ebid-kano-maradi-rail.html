EBID approves $100m for Kano–Maradi rail equipment

The ECOWAS Bank for Investment and Development has approved a US$100 million facility for rolling stock and operational equipment for Phase II of the Kano–Maradi Standard Gauge Railway Project.

Executive brief

EBID's board approved the facility at its 28 September 2026 meeting in Lomé, according to a bank announcement published on 30 September. The allocation is part of four approved operations totalling US$390 million across West Africa. EBID identifies the rail funding specifically for the acquisition of rolling stock and operational equipment for Phase II.

The Kano–Maradi railway is intended to strengthen transport links and support trade and mobility between Nigeria and Niger. EBID has not stated a disbursement date, supplier award, equipment quantities or delivery schedule in this announcement.

Why this is APL-worthy

This is a named, nine-figure financing approval for the equipment needed to operate a cross-border rail corridor. It moves the project financing picture beyond civil works and toward the assets required for service.

What to watch

Loan documentation and disbursement, rolling-stock procurement, delivery milestones and the construction status of Phase II will determine when the approval translates into operating capacity.
